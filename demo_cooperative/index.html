Cooperative Aerial Vehicles for Payload Transportation

NYU AIR Lab

New York University, NYU Abu Dhabi

Overview

This project focuses on the development of cooperative aerial systems capable of transporting arbitrary-shaped payloads using multiple drones. The architecture supports both centralized and decentralized flight control, allowing adaptive, modular, and reliable performance under varying constraints. The overall goal is to enable intelligent multi-drone systems for real-world transport applications.

  • Centralized and Decentralized Control: Flexibly switchable for mission needs.
  • Optimal Control Allocation: Increases energy efficiency and flight time.
  • Modular Payload Structures: Designed for adaptability and scalability.
  • Online Adaptive Control: Real-time adjustments to dynamic changes and disturbances.
