Quadruped Robot Navigation in Unknown Environments

NYU AIR Lab

New York University Abu Dhabi

Overview

This project focuses on enabling quadruped robots to navigate safely and efficiently in complex, unknown environments. The system integrates real-time perception and planning to adapt to dynamically changing terrains.

  • Control Barrier Functions (CBFs): Hierarchical planning with formal safety guarantees.
  • Embodied Navigation: Adjusts to unfamiliar and evolving environments without prior mapping.
  • Robust Performance: Designed to ensure stability, obstacle avoidance, and mission success.
