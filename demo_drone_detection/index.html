Drone Detection & Geofencing

New York University Abu Dhabi

Overview

This project focuses on detecting and tracking unauthorized or evasive drones in restricted airspaces using real-time deep learning and multi-modal sensing. By integrating advanced RGB cameras with Pan-Tilt-Zoom (PTZ) mechanisms and optionally RGB-T sensors, our system ensures wide-range, high-resolution coverage and precise geofencing capabilities.

To maintain long-term tracking under dynamic flight conditions, we fuse deep learning models with homography estimation and optical flow tracking. The system achieves detection speeds of up to 30 FPS and enables enforcement of virtual boundaries through responsive geofencing protocols.

Key Features

  • Pan-Tilt-Zoom (PTZ) RGB Camera: Tracks fast-moving drones over large areas.
  • 30 FPS Real-Time Deep Learning: Detects drone signatures with high accuracy.
  • Fusion of Deep Learning + Optical Flow + Homography: Enables robust long-term tracking.
  • RGB-T Integration: Improves detection in low-visibility or thermal signature-rich scenarios.
