LLM-Policy Training for Humanoid Manipulation Actions

NYU AIR Lab

New York University Abu Dhabi

Overview

This project explores the integration of Large Language Models (LLMs) into policy training for humanoid robots, enabling adaptive and efficient manipulation in real-world environments. Our approach leverages the reasoning power of LLMs in combination with sensory input for precise, context-aware action generation.

  • LLM-Driven Policies: Use of large language models to produce intelligent, contextual manipulation behavior.
  • Multi-Modal Integration: Combine language and sensor modalities to enhance perception and decision-making.
  • Real-Time Adaptability: Quickly adapt to dynamic changes in the environment with minimal latency.
