H²-COMPACT: Human–Humanoid Co-Manipulation

NYU AIR Lab

New York University Abu Dhabi

Overview

H²-COMPACT (Human–Humanoid Co-Manipulation via Adaptive Contact Trajectories) is a collaborative manipulation framework designed to enable humanoid robots to work physically alongside humans in shared object handling tasks. The system focuses on real-time adaptive trajectory generation for humanoid arms and body, synchronized with human movements.

Through multi-modal sensor fusion (including vision, force, and proprioception) and reinforcement-based trajectory optimization, the humanoid agent learns to anticipate and adapt to human intention—allowing for smooth, efficient, and safe object transportation or assembly tasks. H²-COMPACT opens up new possibilities for human-robot teaming in logistics, assistive care, and manufacturing.

Key Features

  • Adaptive Trajectory Generation: Contact-aware control for robust human–robot synchronization.
  • Real-Time Intention Prediction: Predicts human intent from body motion and force signals.
  • Force-Sensitive Planning: Incorporates feedback to modulate humanoid contact forces.
  • Application Agnostic: Applicable to load lifting, co-carrying, and assistive support.
